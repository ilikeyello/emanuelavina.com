How do I get my purchase back on a new phone?

Open Pro Garage from the Profile screen and tap Restore Purchases at the bottom. The unlock is tied to the Apple Account that bought it, so as long as you are signed in to that account it comes straight back. Family Sharing members restore it the same way.

Do I need an account to use cartab?

No. You can add a vehicle, log service, run projects, set reminders and export a record without ever signing in.

Signing in with Apple is what backs your written records up and lets them follow you to a new phone. It is also required before buying the unlock, because the purchase is stored against your account rather than against one phone — otherwise a lost phone would mean a lost purchase.

Does it work without a signal?

Yes. Everything is saved to your phone first and syncs when you are back online. Nothing in the app waits on a connection.

Where are my photos kept?

On the free version, on your device only — they are never uploaded. Unlocking Pro Garage backs them up at full quality so they return when you sign in on another phone.

Your written records — vehicles, service entries, projects, parts and costs — sync for everyone, free or not. Losing years of records to a dead phone should not be a paid feature.

How do I export my records?

  • One vehicle, as a document. Open its service history, tap the gear icon, then Export Service Record. That produces a colour-coded PDF with the full history, parts, project timelines and costs — the thing to hand a buyer or a mechanic.
  • Everything, as data. Profile → Export Vehicle Work Logs produces a CSV of every vehicle for a spreadsheet.

How do I delete a vehicle, or my whole account?

A single vehicle: open it, tap the gear icon, then Delete Vehicle. Everything: Profile → Delete Account.

Both ask you to confirm first. Deleting your account permanently removes your profile, vehicles, service history, projects and photos from the servers.

What does the unlock include?

Unlock Unlimited is $2.99, once:

  • Every vehicle you own, instead of one
  • Photo backup at full quality
  • Family Sharing, so one purchase covers up to six people

It is not a subscription. One payment, no ads, and nothing else to buy.
